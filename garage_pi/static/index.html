Calibration

Parked cars are detected by comparing the radar image with an empty-garage reference, using per-stall thresholds fitted from recordings where you tell the system which stalls are occupied. Each step records about 40 seconds. Nobody should be in the garage while it records.

  1. Empty garage, doors as they usually are when a car arrives (open). No cars, no people.
  2. Empty garage, doors closed. Combined with step 1 so door position never looks like a car.
  3. Cars parked. Set which stalls have a car, then record. Do several combinations - especially each stall empty while its neighbours are parked (that is where the radar's beam blur matters most).
  4. Fit the thresholds from all calibration recordings (takes a minute on the Pi).

Settings

Saved to config.json. GPIO, LED, MQTT and port changes apply after restarting the service (sudo systemctl restart garage-radar).

Indicator MAC addresses

Saved in the detector's flash, so they survive power loss. Each indicator logs its MAC address at startup. Saving a changed address restarts the detector for a few seconds. Leave blank for a stall with no indicator.

Indicator distances

Where each indicator's LED bar works between. At the full-bar distance or farther the whole bar is green; it shrinks to one LED at the stop distance, and closer than that the bar is red. The detector keeps these and the indicator takes them over, and saves them in its own flash, on its next wake (up to the request interval). Leave both blank to go back to the indicator's built-in values.
